The Information Age

A Major Era of History

The Importance of Information Age

The Information Age is the period when computers, the internet, and digital technology became a major part of everyday life.

When Did It Begin?

The Information Age began during the late 20th century as computers and digital communication became more useful in everyday life.

Important Technology

  • Computers
  • The Internet
  • Smartphones
  • Social Media
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • GPS and Navigation Systems
  • Video Game Consoles (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch)
  • Tablets
  • Smartwatches

Why Is It Important?

The Information Age has transformed the way people communicate, work, learn, and conduct business around the world. Technology has become an essential part of our everyday lives, connecting people and information faster than ever before. From smartphones and digital communication to advanced systems used in the workplace, technology continues to shape how we solve problems, collaborate, and make decisions. In many ways, technology has moved from simply being a tool we use to becoming an integral part of how we work and live.

How I Developed This Code

I developed this code from scratch using Notepad, where I manually wrote the HTML and CSS. I used HTML to create the structure of the webpage and CSS to control the colors, spacing, layout, and overall appearance. I also used an external CSS file to separate the styling from the code, making the project easier to read and organize. I copied the code I had already created and transferred the project files to GitHub so I could publish the webpage and provide a live link. GitHub allowed me to share my work online without rebuilding the website. During this step, the only adjustments I made were to the images. I then reviewed the published webpage to confirm that the code and images displayed as expected.

My Process of Creating the Code

My approach was to start with an idea, break it down into manageable components, build the structure, apply the styling, and review the results in a browser. I paid attention to how the HTML elements were organized, how the external CSS file affected the presentation, and how the images supported the code. This project reinforced the importance of understanding the fundamentals, paying attention to details, and being willing to learn new tools. Although I originally wrote my code independently in Notepad, I learned how to use GitHub to publish and share the finished project. To me, that is part of engineering: understanding what you build, adapting when a process is unfamiliar, and finding a way to bring an idea to completion.

Why I Chose HTML and CSS?

HTML has always been my first language, and coding has always had a place in my heart. I chose HTML and CSS for this project because they allowed me to demonstrate my understanding of web development fundamentals while creating something functional from the ground up. HTML defines the structure and content of my webpage, while CSS controls its visual presentation. I wanted to keep the process straightforward and focus on writing, organizing, and understanding my own code rather than relying on a framework to do the work for me.

What Made Me Want To Code?

Growing up, I always had a passion for technology. From the time I was a little girl, I found myself helping others resolve their technical issues. In middle school, my teachers were amazed that I could solve technical problems they had submitted support tickets for. That curiosity followed me into high school, where I served as a Technical Intern for DeSoto ISD during my junior and senior years while also attending the Technology Academy. During that time, the late Congresswoman Eddie Bernice Johnson asked me to code an app for her program, and I was later awarded second place for my work.

During that experience, one of my high school teachers introduced me to both software and hardware, which became a turning point for me. I instantly fell in love with coding and became even more interested in understanding how computers work, from their physical components to the software that brings them to life. That passion has stayed with me ever since. Being able to take an idea, write instructions, and watch those instructions become something real still amazes me. Knowing that I can create something by putting my mind to it is one of the things I love most about coding. These experiences have strengthened my passion for technology and helped shape the path I continue to pursue today.

Why I Chose the Information Age

I chose the Information Age because it allows me to connect my current role as a History teacher with my passion for technology. Through this topic, I can explore how technological advancements have transformed the way people communicate, access information, learn, work, and conduct business. I wanted to create a website that not only demonstrates my understanding of web development but also highlights the impact technology has on our daily lives.
